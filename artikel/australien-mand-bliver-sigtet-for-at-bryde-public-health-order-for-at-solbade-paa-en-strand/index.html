Australien: Mand bliver sigtet for at bryde "Public Health Order" - ved at solbade på en strand

Del
XFacebookTelegramE-mail

En Australsk mand bliver sigtet for at bryde "Public Health Order" loven i Australien, som er et af de mest nedlukkede lande i verden. Store dele af landet er hårdt ramt af lowkdowns og befolkningen bliver konstant presset af deres regering. Regeringen har gennemført en række strenge krav og love. Eksempelvis har frontlinearbejderne fået valget mellem at lade sige vaccinere eller blive fyret og få en bøde. Flere steder må borgerne ikke forlade hjemmet med mindre en 'reasonable excuse' haves. Solbadning og sang i kirken er forbudt. Læs et uddrag af loven, under videoen.

Entering and leaving Greater Sydney

People cannot enter or leave Greater Sydney without a reasonable excuse. Reasonable excuses include to go to the person's place of residence, work, childcare, attending school or an educational institutions, for medical care or donating blood, as a carer, on compassionate grounds, for family contact arrangements, for emergencies and to avoid risks to the person, for animal welfare, to fulfil legal obligations, to access public services, moving home or inspecting property, transporting household members, moving to temporary accommodation, religious functions (for religious leaders only), and attending a wedding service, funeral or memorial service.

A person may enter Greater Sydney to obtain goods or services if the goods or services are for the personal needs of the person's household or for other household purposes, including for vulnerable persons or pets, and the goods or services, or equivalent goods or services, are not reasonably available outside Greater Sydney.

A person may leave Greater Sydney to obtain goods or services if the goods or services are for the personal needs of the person's household or for other household purposes, including for vulnerable persons or pets, and the goods or services, or equivalent goods or services, are not reasonably available in Greater Sydney.

A reasonable excuse does not include exercise or outdoor recreation.
